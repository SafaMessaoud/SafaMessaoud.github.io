I am an AI Scientist at the [Qatar Computing Research Institute (QCRI)](https://www.hbku.edu.qa/en/qcri) at Hamad Bin Khalifa University in Doha, Qatar. I am also the Co-founder and Co-director of [MenaML](https://www.menaml.com/), the Middle East and North Africa Machine Learning Winter School — a regional initiative that brings together students, researchers, and leading scientists from across MENA and the world to build a thriving ML community in the region.

I obtained my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) in 2021, advised by [Prof. Alexander G. Schwing](https://alexander-schwing.de/). My doctoral thesis, *Toward More Scalable Structured Models*, focused on combining structured prediction, reinforcement learning, and energy-based models for vision and decision-making problems. Before that, I earned an M.S. from Virginia Tech and an M.S. and B.S. from the Technical University of Munich, with a research stay at UC Berkeley.

Research interests
======
My research is in **machine learning** at the intersection of generative modeling, reinforcement learning, and trustworthy AI. I am particularly interested in:

- **Generative models & energy-based methods** — diffusion and score-based models, Stein Variational Gradient Descent, and structured/energy-based formulations for inference.
- **Reinforcement learning** — max-entropy RL, learned heuristics for combinatorial inference, and RL for structured prediction.
- **Large language models** — Arabic-centric and multimodal LLMs (recent work on [Fanar](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13944)).
- **Safe and robust AI** — adversarial training, the geometry of robustness, and the robustness-generalization tradeoff.
- **Healthcare analytics** — applying ML to electronic health records, transcriptomics, and biomedical signals.
