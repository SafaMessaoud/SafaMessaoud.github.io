I am an AI Scientist at the [Qatar Computing Research Institute (QCRI)](https://www.hbku.edu.qa/en/qcri) at Hamad Bin Khalifa University in Doha, Qatar. I am also a Co-founder and Co-director at [MenaML](https://www.menaml.com/), the Middle East and North Africa Machine Learning Winter School — a UK-registered NGO that brings together students, researchers, and leading scientists from across MENA and the world to build a thriving ML community in the region.

I obtained my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) in 2021, advised by [Prof. Alexander G. Schwing](https://alexander-schwing.de/). Before that, I earned an M.S. from Virginia Tech and an M.S. and B.S. from the Technical University of Munich, and spent one year as a visiting scholar at UC Berkeley.
