**RoBee** is a community-based platform for **collecting, merging, and sharing career roadmaps** — the goal is to be the **Google Maps for career journeys**.

People navigating their professional lives — students choosing a field, early-career professionals weighing a pivot, returning workers re-entering an industry — rarely have a clear map of the routes others have taken. RoBee turns individual journeys into a navigable, crowd-sourced atlas:

- **Collect** — contributors document the milestones, decisions, and detours that shaped their career path.
- **Merge** — similar roadmaps are aligned and combined into shared "trunk" trajectories with optional branches and side-trips.
- **Share** — anyone can browse, follow, or remix a roadmap relevant to where they're going.

The platform is being built as part of the *Mentorship Platform* project funded by the **QRDI Social Innovation Fund** (8th cycle).
