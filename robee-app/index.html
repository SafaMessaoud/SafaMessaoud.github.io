RoBee — short for Roadmap Building exchange — is a community-driven platform for collecting, merging, and sharing career roadmaps. Think Google Maps for career journeys.

Join the waiting list → How it works

Like a map for where you're going

Navigating a career — choosing a field, weighing a pivot, re-entering an industry — usually means guessing the route. RoBee turns individual journeys into a navigable atlas: contributors share the milestones and detours that shaped their path, and similar journeys merge into graphs that reveal all the possible routes toward a goal, just like a navigation system shows alternate paths between two points.

How RoBee works

A more scalable approach to mentorship — one that augments human guidance rather than replacing it.

🧭

Collect

Contributors document the milestones, decisions, and detours that shaped their journey toward a goal.

🔗

Merge

Similar roadmaps align and combine into graphs that visualize every trajectory toward similar destinations.

🤝

Mentor

Hold mentorship sessions anchored to a shared roadmap — discuss decisions and refine paths together.
